# Modular Synthesizer

If you've ever looked into synthesizers, you know that they're hella expensive. Modular synthesizers are even more expensive. Dreams setups that I've created in [Modulargrid](https://modulargrid.net/) reached 3000 dollars or more in cost, so I decided to build my own. Luckily, the Eurorack DIY community is quite active, following a DIY synthesizer tradition that has been around for decades. The wealth of knowledge and expertise was very valuable when designing and layout out my own unit.

This is a Eurorack synth currently in the experimentation stage (testing out circuits). 

I spent a lot of time planning every detail while waiting to save enough money up to buy my tools. Now that I do have my tools, I will test out some freely available schematics before producing the PCBs and assembling them.

There are a few modules that I will design myself, because they either do not exist in the exact form I want, or because they're closed-source. Luckily, my ideas were not too novel, meaning I was able to analyze existing implementations, as their workings are rather transparent. When completed, they will be published as open hardware, because not enough people have created or published these particular modules that I desire.

## Why Eurorack?

Simply put, it is very well-documented, and my design iterations went from custom fixed synths to custom modular to Eurorack, because I want to retain compatibility with modules I may buy someday if needed. Additionally, as mentioned earlier, the DIY community is very active. Many knowledgable people publish their designs for free, which I really appreciate. DIY kits for sale have been increasing in popularity, too, but they are still a bit expensive for me, and I like a challenge. This project is what got me into electronics, in fact.

If this project is successful, I might take on something else, like Buchla or separate drum machines. Buchla is even more epensive, but it has a dedicated following that has documented modules and introduced new ones.

Other formats exist too, like Kosmo, but again, it doesn't hurt to go with something widely available like Eurorack.
