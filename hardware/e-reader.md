# E-Reader

An ESP32-S3-based e-book reader. Its parts and general circuitry have been determined. I began working on the firmware at some point but I have
postponed the project due to the difficulties of acquiring a display.

I was planning a lot with this, but finding a suitable E-ink display proved to be very difficult where I live, and importing one is too expensive.

In the end, I figured that Inkplate might be a good choice. It even comes with the ESP32 built-in. If I ever buy one of these, I could try the
[existing E-reader software](https://github.com/turgu1/EPub-InkPlate) I found for the Inkplate specifically, in which case I could
fork it as needed, but I still want to undertake something of my own design at some point.

I will upload the code when it's presentable and the schematics when they're made and tested.
